How to play

FLOWDROP is a six-lane rhythm game that turns your music into playable charts. Import up to 30 audio files and choose Easy, Normal, or Hard for each track. Your music is processed in your browser and stays on your device.

Hit each note as it reaches the judgment line with S D F / J K L, or tap the six pads on a touchscreen. Hold long notes until their tails reach the line, and press both lanes together for double hits. Aim for a full combo and a perfect score of 100,000.

Start with the built-in practice beat, or bring your own music. You can save a chart as JSON and import it again with the same version of the song. In Settings, you can adjust scroll speed, timing offset, lane view, and key sounds.
